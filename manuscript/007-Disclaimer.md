# Disclaimer

This is a book about a cryptocurrency wallet called Signet. The software is provided with absolutely no warranty. At the time of writing, it's still under development.

In the book, we talk about buying Tezos coins (tez) and NFTs, and swapping tokens. The activities are shown for educational purposes. Do not take anything written in this book as investment advice.

Cryptocurrencies and blockchains are decentralised - there is no bank to help you. If you lose your secret keys, you lose access to your funds. If someone else gets hold of your secret keys, they can take your funds and assets. It's important that you back up your keys regularly as nobody can help you if you lose them.

For tax purposes, you should record the monetary value of any purchases of cryptoassets, including coins and NFTs. You should record any swaps of tokens. Also, staking rewards usually count as income. When you sell cryptoassets or receive them as income, you may need to let your tax authority know or declare any gain (or loss) on your tax return. Legislation differs between countries.

Before trying anything on Tezos's production network, *Mainnet*, it's prudent to try it on one of the test networks first.

The addresses on the Tezos blockchain used in the book are shown for demonstration purposes only. Please do not send funds to them.

As the Signet software develops, the user interface may change. Sometimes the changes will be small, other times large. It's possible that the book will be out of step with the current release of the software, although efforts will be made to keep it up to date.
