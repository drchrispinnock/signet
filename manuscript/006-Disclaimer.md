# Disclaimer

This is a book about a cryptocurrency wallet. The Signet software is provided with absolutely no warranty. At the time of writing it's still under development.

In the book, we talk about buying Tezos coins, NFTs and swapping tokens. The activities are shown for educational purposes. Do not take anything written in this book as investment advice.

For tax purposes, you should record the monetary value of any purchases of cryptoassets including coins and NFTs. You should also record any swaps of tokens. When you sell cryptoassets, you may need to let your tax authority know or declare it as a gain (or loss) on your tax return. Legislation differs between countries.

Before trying anything on Tezos's production network, *mainnet*, it's prudent to try it on one of the test networks first.

As the Signet software develops, the user interface may change. Sometimes this will be slight and other times the change will be big. It's possible that the book will be out of sync with the release of software, although efforts will be made to keep it up to date.
